How it works

Basics

  • Start or stop with the pink button or the space bar.
  • Extra tools (Measure grid, Speed trainer, Silent measures, Count-in, Swing, Polymeter, Presets) stay hidden until you turn them on in Options. Turning one off resets it.
  • Tap sets the tempo from your taps (at least two).
  • Save keeps your settings in this browser. Reset restores the defaults.
  • Digital shows lit beats, Physical shows a swinging metronome.

Subdivision

  • Choose how each beat is split. Subdivision notes play softer than the beat.

Custom rhythm

  • Pick a grid, then tap slots: note, accented note, rest.
  • Add rhythm puts it with the other subdivisions. Use the pink × on it to delete it.

Measure grid

  • Tick Play the grid to loop through the measures, each beat using its own rhythm.
  • Drag a subdivision onto a beat, or select one and tap a beat.
  • Drag a beat onto another to copy it. Drop a subdivision on a measure number to fill the measure.
  • Copy duplicates a measure, Delete removes it, Add measure appends one.
  • Export MIDI saves the grid (in this viewer it comes as a .zip holding the .mid file). Import MIDI loads a .mid file into the grid: tempo, time signature, rhythms and accents.

Accent pattern

  • Sets the strength of each beat: strong, medium, weak or mute. A muted beat is silent, subdivision included.
  • It applies to every measure of the grid.

Practice tools

  • Speed trainer raises (or lowers) the tempo by a step every N measures until the target.
  • Silent measures plays N measures, then mutes M while the visuals keep going.
  • Count-in plays clicks before the grid starts. Swing delays the off-beat notes (100% is a triplet feel).
  • Polymeter adds a second click that splits each measure into 2 to 7 equal parts.
  • In the grid, each measure has a repeat count and an optional tempo (empty = global).
  • Random gaps mutes measures by chance. Session timer stops after N minutes. Timing trainer shows how early or late your taps are (Tap or T). Subdivision mix sets their volume and sound. Setlist chains saved presets for N measures each. Display and latency adds a beat-1 flash/vibration, keeps the screen on, and delays visuals to match Bluetooth audio.
  • Presets live in this browser. A share code holds every setting: paste it elsewhere to load it.

Sound

  • Sound type and pitch change the click. Volume is the slider next to tempo.
